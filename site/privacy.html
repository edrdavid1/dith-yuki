What the app sends

Dither Yuki does not collect usage analytics. Your images and project files stay on your computer.

Copies from the Mac App Store and the Microsoft Store do not send files, project data, or bug reports. Those copies have no network client for that purpose. A link you choose to open is handed to your browser.

The direct download can send a bug report only when you choose Report a bug. That report contains the text you type, an optional email address, the app version, and the operating system. It is sent through Web3Forms so we can read it. It does not include your images or project files.

Store purchases are sold by Apple or Microsoft. Payment, tax, and refunds for those copies are handled by the store. The license to use the app remains with David Murashka, operating as L'eco non di Bergamo.
